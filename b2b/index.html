Export Logistics & Incoterms

FOB or CIF. You choose.

Shipping method, port and Incoterms rule are agreed in each written quotation, which names the rule, its edition and the port. All quotations are issued in US dollars (USD).

Incoterms® 2020

FOB — Free On Board

We deliver the goods on board the vessel at the Egyptian port of loading. You arrange and pay main freight and insurance.

  • Best if you have your own freight forwarder.
  • Risk passes to you once the goods are on board.
Incoterms® 2020

CIF — Cost, Insurance & Freight

We arrange sea freight and insurance to your named destination port. Import clearance, duties and taxes remain with you.

  • One price to your port, with no forwarder needed at origin.
  • Risk passes to you once the goods are on board.

Questions importers ask

Good to know.

Are you a factory or a trading company?

We are a manufacturer and exporter of natural leather products, established in 1978.

Can my logo go on the product?

Yes. We engrave your logo on the leather and can discuss private labeling. Send your artwork with the inquiry.

Are the product photos real?

Some images are AI-enhanced or illustrative. Natural hair-on hides vary, so we send photos of your actual pieces before shipment.

Will every piece look identical?

Hair-on cowhide is a natural material, so pattern and exact size vary from hide to hide. For orders that need consistency, we agree an approved sample and grading rules before production.

Which countries do you ship to?

We export to importers wherever trade is legal and compliant. Tell us your country and we confirm the documents and requirements for it.

I want a single piece, not a wholesale order.

Message us on WhatsApp for single pieces. We confirm price, size and delivery in Egypt or abroad.
